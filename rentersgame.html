THE RENTER'S GAME

ABOUT THE GAME

Inspired by a viral Monopoly 2026 sketch I created, I'm developing a board game that reimagines Monopoly for today's housing market. In The Renter's Game, players aren't competing to own the entire board, the first person to own a single piece of property wins.
